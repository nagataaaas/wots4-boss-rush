# Boss Rush

[日本語](README.md) | [Changelog](CHANGELOG.en.md)

> [!NOTE]
> This Mod is for the [Steam version of Way of the Samurai 4](https://store.steampowered.com/app/312780/4/?l=english) only.

Once you have seen the true route's ending, "The Light of Hope", the Specter in the graveyard offers "Fight the strong". Fight the strong of the three factions and of the town one after another, in an empty graveyard.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f9b8cdfe-91cd-4776-8035-d689243c0d03

## How to play

After seeing "The Light of Hope", talk to the Specter in the graveyard, choose "Fight the strong" and pick your opponents. You are taken into an empty graveyard where the strong await, and the gauntlet begins.

| Choice | Unlocked by |
| --- | --- |
| Fight the strong of the Prajnas | Seeing "The Light of Hope" |
| Fight the strong of the Shogunate | Seeing "The Light of Hope" |
| Fight the strong of the foreigners | Seeing "The Light of Hope" |
| Fight the Four Samurai Lords (except Kumagami) | Clearing the occupation "Four Samurai Lords vs. Yakuza Quartet" |
| Fight the Yakuza Quartet | Clearing the occupation "Four Samurai Lords vs. Yakuza Quartet" |
| Fight the Amihama Eight | Clearing the occupation "The Amihama Eight" |

Conditions met in earlier playthroughs count too.

- During a boss rush, you cannot walk out of the graveyard.
- The strong you defeat in a boss rush do not die in the story.

## Installation

### Automatic installation (recommended)

1. Close the game.
2. Download and run [WayOfTheSamurai4ModInstaller.exe](https://github.com/nagataaaas/wots4-mods/releases/latest/download/WayOfTheSamurai4ModInstaller.exe).
   - If "Windows protected your PC" appears, click "More info" → "Run anyway".
   - When asked whether to allow it to make changes as administrator, click "Yes" (it writes to the game folder).
3. Follow the screen and choose `boss_rush` in the list of Mods. Move with Up and Down, choose with Space and confirm with Enter.

The ModLoader is installed along with it. The same steps update the Mod.

## Uninstallation

1. Close the game.
2. Delete the `{game installation folder}/mods/boss_rush/` folder.

The Mod adds nothing to your saves, so they keep working after you remove it.
